### 🎯 The Solution
Your submitted solution:
- Should **leverage a vulnerability on the challenge page**.
- Shouldn't be self-XSS or related to MiTM attacks.
- Should work in the latest version of Google Chrome.
- Should not require more than 1 click from the victim (if applicable).
- Must include:
  - The flag in the format `INTIGRITI{.*}`
  - The payload(s) used
  - Steps to solve (short description / bullet points)
- Should be reported on the [Intigriti platform](https://go.intigriti.com/submit-solution).
